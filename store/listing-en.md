```
Nut & Bolt Sort is a sleek nut sort puzzle with a machined-metal look. Unscrew the colored nuts, move them between threaded bolts, and tighten every color onto its own bolt. It's easy to learn and surprisingly deep: a relaxing brain teaser for a quick break or a long session.

🔩 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a bolt to unscrew its top nut, together with any nuts of the same color right below it.
• Tap another bolt to screw them on: onto the same color, or onto an empty bolt.
• Each bolt holds 4 nuts (some later levels use 5).
• Fill every bolt with a single color to clear the level.

⚙️ FEATURES
• 1000+ levels: a sorting game that starts gentle and ramps up to 12 colors.
• Every level is solvable: each puzzle is checked by a built-in solver before you play it.
• Play offline: no Wi-Fi needed. A true no wifi game for the bus, a flight, or anywhere.
• Free, unlimited Undo, and Restart any time.
• Stuck? Watch an optional ad for a Hint (the solver shows the next move) or a +1 spare bolt.
• Tight levels with just one spare bolt, and bigger 5-nut bolts, for extra challenge.
• Satisfying screw-twist animations, crisp metallic sound effects and light haptics (all can be turned off).
• Earn coins as you play and unlock cosmetic finishes: chrome and neon nuts, brass, copper, black-oxide and titanium bolts, and carbon, blueprint and tread-plate backdrops.
• Color-blind aid: optional marks on every nut.
• No timer and no pressure. Your progress is saved on your device automatically.

🧠 A RELAXING BRAIN TEASER
Nuts and bolts puzzles look simple, but every move counts. Plan ahead, keep a bolt free, and find the right order to sort each color. If you like screw puzzle games, bolt puzzle games, color sort, water sort or ball sort, you'll feel right at home with this offline puzzle game.

💡 TIPS
• A free bolt is your best tool, so try not to fill it too early.
• Finish bolts that are almost sorted first.
• Made a mistake? Tap Undo. Really stuck? Try a Hint, add a spare bolt, or Restart.

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Nut & Bolt Sort is free to play and shows ads. Hints and the spare bolt come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• There are no in-app purchases. Coins can only be earned by playing and are only used for cosmetic finishes.
• No sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/nut-bolt-sort/privacy.html

Download Nut & Bolt Sort and start sorting: unscrew, sort and tighten!
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.0.0):

- **1000+ levels / every level solvable:** levels are generated without limit. Each one is accepted only after the solver finds a replayable solution, and `npm test` regenerates, replays and re-solves levels 1–1000 in CI.
- **Up to 12 colors, 4 or 5 nuts per bolt, tight levels with one spare bolt:** see `levelParams()` in `www/js/logic.js`.
- **Offline:** the game runs fully offline. Ads just don't load without internet.
- **Free unlimited Undo and Restart; Hint and +1 bolt via optional rewarded ads:** correct. The +1 bolt works once per level.
- **Coins are cosmetic only; no in-app purchases:** correct. There is no billing library in the app.
- **No account or sign-up:** correct.
