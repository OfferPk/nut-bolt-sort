**1478 / 4000 characters** (Python `len()`; bullets count as 1)

```
Nut & Bolt Sort is a tactile color-sorting puzzle. Unscrew nuts, move them between bolts and tighten every bolt with a single color. Every level is verified solvable.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a bolt to lift its top nuts, then tap another bolt to drop them
• Nuts land only on an empty bolt or the same color with room to spare
• Undo is free; optional Hint and +1 Bolt help when you are stuck
• Sort every bolt to one color to clear the level

COME BACK EVERY DAY
• A Daily Bolt level that is the same for everyone, plus a 7-day reward calendar
• Rising coin rewards for a 7-day streak (miss a day and it restarts)
• Daily challenge streaks

ACHIEVEMENTS & STATS
• 17 achievements for progress, skill, streaks, daily challenges and collecting
• Stats: games played, best results, coins earned and your longest play streak

SKIN SHOP
• 7 nut finishes, 7 bolt metals and 7 backdrops (Rose Gold, Cobalt, Pearl Coat, Workbench Oak and more)
• Preview before you buy. Everything is cosmetic and bought with coins you earn in the game
• No real-money purchases, no loot boxes, no cash-out

MADE FOR RELAXING
• Plays fully offline
• Friendly first-level tutorial (replay it any time from Settings)
• Sound and haptics toggles, color-blind friendly options where available

ADS
The game is free and shows ads. Optional rewarded ads play only when you tap a button (for example a hint or a free 24-hour skin try). Full-screen ads only appear between finished games or levels, never at launch.
```
